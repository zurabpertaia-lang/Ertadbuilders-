FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can apply?

Anyone building or planning a company. Founder Institute Georgia focuses on pre-seed teams; the bootcamp is open to beginners and experienced builders.

Is there a fee?

Each program lists its own fee and scholarship options on its page. Ask us if cost is a barrier.

Do I need a technical background?

No for Founder Institute and workshops. The AI-native bootcamp includes an onboarding week for those new to coding.

How do I become a partner or sponsor?

Send us a message through the contact form and we'll share the partnership deck.
